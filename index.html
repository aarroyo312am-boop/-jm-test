First time here?

Read this before you pull in.

  1. Come in from Lake Street to 31st Ave. It's the easiest approach for a tractor-trailer.
  2. Slide your tandems all the way forward before the turn. The entrance is tight.
  3. Swing wide from the left lane once oncoming traffic is clear, then follow the orange barrels at idle speed to the back lot.
  4. Walk into the open bay and tell the crew what you need. They'll wave you into the bay.
  5. Look over your unit before you leave. If anything was missed, tell the crew right there.
